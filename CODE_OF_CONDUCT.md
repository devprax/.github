# DevPrax Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

DevPrax aims to be a welcoming community where people can build useful tools and learn from one another. Everyone should be able to participate without harassment, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Community standards

- Treat people with respect and assume good intent while remaining open to feedback about the impact of your actions.
- Give constructive, specific feedback about ideas and work. Respect differences in experience and perspective.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes, apologize when appropriate, and adjust your behavior.
- Respect people's time, privacy, and boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, targeted insults, doxxing, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable. Neither are spam, repeated disruption, or retaliation against someone who raises a concern or participates in a review.

## Scope

This code applies in DevPrax repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other official community spaces. It also applies when someone represents DevPrax publicly.

## Reporting concerns

DevPrax has not yet designated a private code-of-conduct reporting contact. Do not post incident details, personal information, or evidence in a public issue or discussion.

If you are comfortable doing so, ask a maintainer to arrange a private reporting channel without sharing incident details publicly. Wait until a private channel has been agreed before sending sensitive information. This is a way to request a channel, not an existing confidential reporting service.

For abusive behavior on GitHub, you can also use [GitHub's abuse reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). Reports to GitHub are handled by GitHub and are separate from DevPrax moderation.

Once a private channel is available, useful information includes what happened, where and when it occurred, relevant links or evidence, and any immediate concerns or preferred way to follow up. Share only what you feel comfortable sharing. If a concern involves a maintainer, request review by an uninvolved maintainer where one is available.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content that violates this code and may restrict participation. Depending on severity and repetition, action can include a request to change behavior, a warning, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban. Serious incidents may warrant immediate restrictions.

Maintainers should review concerns fairly, protect reporter privacy, and share report information only with those who need it to address the concern. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. Retaliation is itself a violation of this code.

A person affected by a moderation decision may request reconsideration through an agreed private channel, preferably with an uninvolved maintainer where available.
